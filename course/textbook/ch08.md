## Working notes

Bag-of-words regression maps each document to its vocabulary counts. Estimate document frequencies on training documents for inductive evaluation. LSA uses singular vectors of a term–document matrix. Skip-gram learns embeddings instead by distinguishing observed context pairs from alternatives.
