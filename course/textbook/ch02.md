## Working notes

For ratings y and design matrix X, least squares minimizes ||y−Xθ||². Encode categories with indicator columns and combine these with numeric or nonlinear features. MSE penalizes large deviations more strongly than MAE. Compare both to a constant prediction before interpreting a fitted coefficient.
