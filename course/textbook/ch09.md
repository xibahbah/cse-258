# Chapter 9 · Personalized Models of Visual Data

Uses image representations for visual preference and compatibility. An item can go well with another even when their appearance differs.

[Textbook · PDF pp. 269–289](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~jmcauley/pml/pml_book.pdf#page=269)

## Working notes

A visual preference model can map a precomputed image vector into a smaller space and compare it with a user representation. Visual compatibility predicts whether two items belong together. Its labels and negative examples therefore differ from those for visual similarity.
