## pp. 1–16 · Prediction problems

Specify a unit of observation, a target available for training, and inputs that will also be known at prediction time. Social links, product opinions, and activity histories support different tasks.

## pp. 17–41 · Course map

Regression predicts a quantity, classification predicts a category, and recommendation conditions a score on both user and item. Later units add timestamps, language, and relationships.

## pp. 42–65 · Project examples and logistics

Past projects illustrate how to turn a broad domain into a measurable prediction. For current deliverables use the Fall 2026 handout.
