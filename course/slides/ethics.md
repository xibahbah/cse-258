## pp. 1–19 · Concentration and exposure

Measure both what each user sees and how total exposure is distributed over creators or items. A diverse individual list can coexist with concentrated platform-wide exposure.

## pp. 20–43 · Diversification

Maximal marginal relevance chooses another item by balancing relevance against similarity to items already selected. Reranking changes exposure without retraining the base predictor.

## pp. 44–56 · Beyond accuracy

Serendipity combines relevance and unexpectedness. Calibration compares the attribute distribution in recommendations with the user’s historical distribution.

## pp. 57–95 · Fairness and bias

Different fairness criteria use different denominators. Specify whose outcomes are being compared and whether the population is users, creators, or items. The book and marketing studies examine exposure and historical bias.
