## pp. 1–24 · Conversational recommenders

Separate fluent responses from successful item suggestions. The examples compare conversational datasets, popularity effects, language-model knowledge, and retrieval or collaborative signals.

## pp. 25–37 · Generative retrieval

Represent items by sequences of identifiers and train a sequence model to generate a next item. Semantic IDs can share structure across items, but a representation useful for reconstruction may be weak for recommendation.
