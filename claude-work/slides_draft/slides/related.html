Section 5 · Related work

How our results line up with the literature

EASE ahead of matrix factorization matches Steck (WWW 2019) on MovieLens-20M, Netflix, and Million Song.

The gap shrinks once ALS is tuned matches Rendle et al. (RecSys 2022): properly tuned iALS is competitive.

BPR strong on AUC, weak on top-N fits its design (Rendle et al., UAI 2009) and uniform-negative results (Rendle & Freudenthaler, WSDM 2014).

Where we differ: prior Steam work (Kang & McAuley, ICDM 2018) predicts the next item from time-ordered reviews. Our file has no timestamps, so levels are not comparable.

Evaluation choices follow Krichene and Rendle (KDD 2020) on sampled metrics and Ferrari Dacrema et al. (RecSys 2019) on tuned baselines.
