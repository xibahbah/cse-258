Section 5 · Conclusions and limits

What we would defend, and what we would not

Defend

EASE > ALS > the rest on top-10 metrics. It held under a second implementation, four splits, and a different hidden game.

Most of an untuned gap between EASE and ALS is a tuning artifact.

Distrust

The absolute level of any number: it depends on which game is hidden.

Any claim about the next game a user will play. That needs timestamps.

Future work: a time-aware split from review timestamps, and content features for cold-start games.
