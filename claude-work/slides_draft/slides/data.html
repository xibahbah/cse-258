users and 3,570 games after an iterative 5-core / 20-core filter.

Source: McAuley Lab Steam Video Game and Bundle Data; profile crawl from O'Neill et al., IMC 2016.

Repeated profiles matter: left unmerged, one copy's hidden game would sit in the other copy's training row.
